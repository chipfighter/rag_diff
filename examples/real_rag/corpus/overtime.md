# Overtime Policy

Overtime must be approved in advance by your direct manager. Unapproved overtime will not be compensated.

On regular weekdays, overtime is paid at 1.5 times the normal hourly rate for any hours worked beyond eight hours in a day.

On weekends, overtime is paid at 2 times the normal hourly rate. This applies to both Saturday and Sunday work.

On public holidays, overtime is paid at 3 times the normal hourly rate. Holiday overtime also requires written approval from a department head.
