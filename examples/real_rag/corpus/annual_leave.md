# Annual Leave Policy

Annual leave is granted based on length of service. The leave year runs from January to December.

Employees with less than two years of service receive 10 days of annual leave per year.

Employees with two to five years of service receive 15 days of annual leave per year.

Employees with more than five years of service receive 20 days of annual leave per year.

Unused annual leave may be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Any remaining balance is forfeited.
