# Travel Expense Policy

Employees who travel for business may claim reimbursement for approved expenses.

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the trip. Claims submitted after 30 days may be rejected.

All claims must include itemized receipts. Expenses without receipts will not be reimbursed.

Flights must be booked in economy class. The daily meal allowance while traveling is 50 dollars per day.
