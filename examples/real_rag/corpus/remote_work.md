# Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely with prior approval from their manager. Remote work is a privilege, not a guarantee.

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. At least 2 days per week must be spent in the office.

Core working hours for all remote employees are 10:00 to 16:00. Employees must be reachable during core hours.

Remote employees are responsible for maintaining a secure and reliable internet connection.
