# Customer Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund according to the time elapsed since purchase.

Within the first 30 days of purchase, customers are entitled to a full refund to their original payment method.

Between 31 and 90 days after purchase, customers may receive store credit equal to the purchase price, but not a cash refund.

After 90 days, all purchases are final and no refund or store credit will be issued.
